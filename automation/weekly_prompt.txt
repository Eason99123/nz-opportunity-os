APPLICATION AVAILABILITY

For internships, jobs, scholarships, research programmes, and other opportunities requiring an application:

Before returning the opportunity, check for evidence that it is still actionable.

Look for:
- an accessible application page
- an Apply or application action
- a future or current deadline
- current programme dates
- wording showing applications or registrations are open

Look for closure signals such as:
- no longer accepting applications
- applications closed
- position filled
- job no longer available
- requisition unavailable
- page not found
- expired
- removed listing
- past deadline

Do not describe an opportunity as open when the available evidence shows that it is closed, expired, removed, or unavailable.

If availability cannot be confidently confirmed, the opportunity may still be returned when it is otherwise strong, but describe the uncertainty accurately in Next Step.
